# Skill: Tone of Voice — Ronald Scheffers

## Voice Characteristics

| Trait | How it sounds |
|-------|---------------|
| **Confident** | States capabilities plainly. No hedging, no "I think" or "maybe". |
| **Concise** | No filler words. Every word earns its place. |
| **Active** | "I build" not "systems are built". First person, direct. |
| **Outcome-focused** | Emphasize results over process. What changes for them? |
| **Slightly provocative** | Challenge assumptions. Make them think. |

---

## Writing Guidelines

### Do

- Start sentences with verbs when possible
- Use short sentences for impact
- Break up long ideas into fragments
- Let punctuation create rhythm
- End with the most important word

### Don't

- Use corporate jargon or buzzwords
- Over-explain or add caveats
- Use passive voice
- Write long, complex sentences
- Sound salesy or pushy

## Copy Examples

### Headlines (punchy, provocative)

```
I make machines do the work. So humans can do the thinking.
What could you automate?
Intelligence by design.
```

### Descriptions (clear, benefit-focused)

```
Repetitive tasks eliminated. Manual processes made obsolete.
Your team freed to do real work.

Identifying where intelligence can transform your operations.
From quick wins to enterprise transformation.

Connecting your tools, data, and AI into seamless,
automated workflows.
```

### CTAs (action-oriented)

```
Get in touch
Let's talk
What could you automate?
```

## Word Bank

### Use These Words

| Category | Words |
|----------|-------|
| Action | Build, architect, design, implement, integrate, automate, transform, eliminate, streamline, connect |
| Quality | Intelligent, seamless, reliable, elegant, precise, clean, scalable |
| Outcome | Results, impact, efficiency, clarity, freedom, focus |

### Avoid These Words

| Word | Why |
|------|-----|
| Revolutionary | Overused, sounds like marketing |
| Cutting-edge | Cliché |
| Synergy | Corporate jargon |
| Leverage | Buzzword |
| Best-in-class | Generic superlative |
| World-class | Same as above |
| Utilize | Just say "use" |
| Solutions | Vague, overused |
| AI Agency | Never mention explicitly |
| Automation Agency | Never mention explicitly |

## Sentence Patterns

### The Fragment
Short. Punchy. Memorable.
> "Complex systems made simple."

### The Contrast
Set up tension, then resolve.
> "I make machines do the work. So humans can do the thinking."

### The List of Three
Build rhythm with triplets.
> "Repetitive tasks eliminated. Manual processes made obsolete. Your team freed to do real work."

### The Question
Challenge the reader directly.
> "What could you automate?"
> "Your competitors are automating. Are you?"

### The Definition
Reframe a concept.
> "Intelligence by design."

## Tone by Section

| Section | Tone | Example |
|---------|------|---------|
| Hero | Bold, memorable | "I make machines do the work." |
| About | Grounded, credible | "I build things that work." |
| Services | Clear, benefit-led | "Repetitive tasks eliminated." |
| Contact | Inviting, provocative | "What could you automate?" |
| Footer | Minimal, functional | Just links and copyright |

## Quick Test

Before publishing copy, ask:

1. Could a competitor say this? (If yes, make it more specific)
2. Is every word necessary? (If not, cut it)
3. Does it sound like a person? (If not, rewrite)
4. Would Ronald actually say this? (If not, adjust)
