# Skill: Brand Identity — Ronald Scheffers

## Who

**Ronald Scheffers** — CTO of an AI Automation Agency.

## Important Context

**The agency role should NEVER be explicitly mentioned.** Do not use phrases like:
- "AI Automation Agency"
- "My agency"
- "Our agency"
- "As a CTO"

Instead, the expertise should be *implied* through:
- Sophisticated, technical design choices
- AI/automation-focused messaging
- Confident, authoritative tone
- The quality of the work itself

## Positioning

Ronald helps businesses automate operations and implement AI solutions. The personal brand should convey:

- **Technical depth** — someone who builds, not just advises
- **Strategic thinking** — sees the big picture
- **Results orientation** — focused on outcomes, not buzzwords
- **Quiet confidence** — lets the work speak

## What Ronald Does (for copy context)

- AI strategy and implementation
- Workflow automation
- Intelligent system integration
- Helping teams eliminate busywork through technology

## Brand Personality

| Trait | Expression |
|-------|------------|
| Confident | States capabilities plainly, no hedging |
| Technical | Understands the details, speaks with precision |
| Forward-thinking | Always looking at what's next |
| Pragmatic | Solutions over theory |
| Understated | Power through simplicity |
